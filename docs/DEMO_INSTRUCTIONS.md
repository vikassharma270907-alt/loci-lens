# Loci Lens — 3-Minute Demo Video Recording Instructions

Follow this second-by-second script when capturing the YouTube demonstration video for Devpost.

---

## Technical Recording Setup
1. **Screen Resolution:** 1920 × 1080 (16:9).
2. **Audio:** Clear microphone narration; system audio captured for `AudioFx` chimes.
3. **Capture Target:** Lens Studio Interactive Preview window (or full IDE screen showing MCP/CLAD console).
4. **Duration Target:** 2 minutes 45 seconds (Must be strictly under 3 minutes).

---

## Second-by-Second Video Script

| Timecode | Visual on Screen | Voiceover Narration |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 - 0:20** | Title card, then quick montage of cluttered mobile flashcard apps vs. empty physical room. | *"Flashcards on a phone isolate information in a flat 2D list. But cognitive science has long proven that our spatial memory is extraordinary. Welcome to Loci Lens—an augmented reality memory palace built for Snap SPECS."* |
| **0:20 - 0:40** | Quick view of `devpost/design-brief.md` generated by the Devpost Learn skill pack and the Lens Studio IDE. | *"We planned Loci Lens with the Devpost Learn skill pack, strictly applying Snap's design rules: drawing in the world, leaving the body free, and keeping interfaces glanceable."* |
| **0:40 - 1:15** | Interactive Preview: Lens launches. User clicks 'Start'. First card cursor appears. User aims at a desk, clicks to pinch. Locus anchor locks in with audio chime. Repeat for doorway, bookshelf, chair, window, table. Peripheral HUD increments: 1/6, 2/6... 6/6. | *"In Place mode, we associate our learning cards with physical locations in the room. Here, I place photosynthesis on my desk, Planck's constant by the charger, and Augustus at the door. Watch the peripheral HUD track our progress at the display margin."* |
| **1:15 - 1:45** | Stroll Mode: Camera navigates the room with WASD. Looking at a card shows its mnemonic icon; approaching within 1.5 m expands the concept. When camera turns away, directional chevron points toward the next locus. | *"In Stroll mode, we walk our memory path. Each locus shows a subtle mnemonic icon. As we approach, the concept expands. If a locus falls out of view, a gentle directional cue guides us forward without cluttering central vision."* |
| **1:45 - 2:20** | Test Mode: Camera approaches the desk. Question prompt appears: 'What is the light-absorbing pigment in photosynthesis?' Answer is occluded. User pinches card: flips to reveal 'Chlorophyll-a'. User pinches 'Got It' (green glow). Moves to card 2: pinches 'Missed' (amber glow). | *"Now the core experience: Test mode. All answers are hidden. We navigate to our locus, read the prompt, and actively recall the answer. A quick pinch flips the card to reveal the answer. We mark 'Got It' or 'Missed', updating our Leitner spaced-repetition store."* |
| **2:20 - 2:45** | Summary Dashboard: Final card answered. Summary plate animates in showing 5/6 (83.3% accuracy), Leitner box breakdown, and 'Retest Missed' button. User clicks 'Retest Missed' to show targeted review loop. | *"When testing completes, the Summary screen breaks down our retention score and Leitner box assignments. With one pinch, we can instantly retest missed cards to guarantee mastery before leaving the palace."* |
| **2:45 - 3:00** | Code / Architecture slide and closing title card with GitHub link. | *"Loci Lens combines cognitive science, the Spectacles Interaction Kit, and closed-loop agentic development to make learning embodied and unforgettable. Thank you."* |
