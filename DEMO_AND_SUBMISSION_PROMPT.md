## 1. Devpost Submission Text

### Project Title
**Loci Lens — Augmented Reality Memory Palace for Snap SPECS**

### Elevator Pitch (140 Characters)
*Loci Lens transforms any room into a spatial memory palace on Snap SPECS, anchoring flashcards to physical loci for embodied active recall.*

### What It Does
Loci Lens is an AR educational experience designed specifically for Snap SPECS that applies the ancient **Method of Loci** mnemonic technique to real-world spaces. Instead of flipping flashcards on a phone, learners place interactive 3D learning cards on real physical surfaces (desks, doorways, bookshelves, chairs). Users then stroll through their environment to reinforce spatial memory pathways, engage in active recall testing where answers remain hidden until explicit interaction, and track retention using a Leitner 3-box spaced-repetition system.

### Who It Is For
Students, medical professionals, language learners, and certification candidates who need to master complex, sequential, or interrelated concepts without staring at flat mobile screens.

### How We Built It
- **Platform:** Snap SPECS via Lens Studio (v5.23).
- **Architecture:** Modular TypeScript architecture (`DeckData`, `LociManager`, `CardObject`, `LocusAnchor`, `PeripheralHUD`, `ReviewStore`, `InputLayer`, `AudioFx`).
- **UI & Interaction:** Built on the **Spectacles Interaction Kit (SIK)** for intuitive pinch-and-dwell mechanics and **Spectacles UI Kit (SUIK)** for high-contrast BackPlate typography.
- **AI-Assisted Workflow:** Planned with the Devpost Learn SPECS skill pack (`plan-lens`), producing an approved `devpost/design-brief.md`, and built using CLAD with automated LEAF verification.

### What We Learned
- **Embodied Cognition in AR:** Cognitive science (Lu et al., Moll & Sykes, Krokos et al.) proves that physical space acts as an indexing structure for memory. Head-worn AR's greatest asset is freeing the hands and body to walk through information.
- **Additive Display Design:** Virtual black is completely transparent on SPECS optical displays. High-contrast translucent backplates and vibrant light typography are essential for legibility.
- **Glanceability:** Keeping secondary status indicators (Peripheral HUD) at the optical margins (18° off-center) prevents cognitive fatigue and central visual clutter.

## 2. YouTube Demo Video Outline (Under 3 Minutes)

- **0:00 - 0:20 (Problem Statement):** Flat mobile flashcards isolate information from reality. The Method of Loci needs physical space.
- **0:20 - 0:40 (Planning & Skill Pack):** Show `devpost/design-brief.md` generated by the Devpost Learn skill pack.
- **0:40 - 1:15 (Place Mode):** Screen recording of Lens Studio preview. Pinning cards to a desk, chair, door, and bookshelf. Peripheral HUD incrementing.
- **1:15 - 1:45 (Stroll Mode):** Walking the room in preview. Showing mnemonic icons and out-of-view directional cues.
- **1:45 - 2:20 (Test Mode):** Active recall in action. Question displayed, answer hidden. Pinch to reveal answer. Marking 'Got It' and 'Missed'.
- **2:20 - 2:45 (Summary Dashboard):** Reviewing retention score, Leitner box assignments, and re-testing missed cards.
- **2:45 - 3:00 (Closing):** Technical summary (CLAD + SIK + SUIK) and call to action.
